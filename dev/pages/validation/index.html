Validation & Bit Parity

1. Frozen reference records

Two committed records fix the arithmetic, so a change that moves every code path together is still detected:

  • Leaf record (test/write_arithmetic_record.jl canonical <out.toml>): IEEE-754 bit strings of leaf fluxes, paired leaf sources and layer tendencies, Float32 and Float64, with and without O₂. Its SHA-256 is locked at 19bcfb73ba7adb1e88f285bbc2679182a861902729216e4948b9d3d350f0d08d, unchanged since the extraction from CanopyColumn.ConservativeExchange (05723a9).
  • Extended record (test/reference/exchange_v0_1_1.toml, written by test/write_reference_record.jl from v0.1.1, commit c35ad8d, and never regenerated from later code): additionally the stem path with dry and transpiring stems (plant_air_sources), the water-energy datum shift of leaf, stem, face and volume streams, steady_top_flux, accumulate_steady_fluxes, surface_static_energy_flux and moist_air_tendencies at gas fraction 1 and below
    1. test/test_reference_record.jl recomputes it and compares bit for bit.

The 0.2.0 open-tracer core reproduces both records exactly: CO₂ and O₂ are carried as the tracers co2 and o2 in the order of the old carbon and oxygen fields. Unary negation uses -field (keeping IEEE -0.0 donor sign bits on empty layers), and division by the gas fraction is fieldwise / ε (exact at ε == 1).

2. Test gates

Pkg.test() (test/runtests.jl) runs:

  • Physical invariants (test_conservative_exchange.jl, test_migration_invariants.jl): interior-face cancellation on a nonuniform grid, donor ledgers, datum shifts on faces and leaves with an operand-based round-off bound, layer subdivision, absent versus computed-zero tracers, empty layers, and independent expressions for the column datum shift (shift × water budget), the surface static-energy flux, transpiring stems and gas fractions below one.
  • Contracts (test_contracts.jl): the precision policy in every constructor and kernel (ArgumentError for Float32 with Float64 and for integers), measure safety, tracer rules, steady accumulation over mixed, abstract and external geometry vectors, record equality identical on ForwardDiff 0.10 and 1.x, the counterexamples of the first review round, and AD contracts (mixed plain and dual inputs, right derivative at an empty layer, rejection of nonfinite derivative components, gradient against central differences).
  • Extension points (test_extension_points.jl): a second tracer set (co2, cos, ch4) through the tagged records, bit-identical to the scalar path, and a third element class with its own geometry defined only in the test.
  • Inference and allocations (test_inference_and_allocations.jl): @inferred, result element type, isbits results and zero allocations for every exported kernel and constructor in Float32 and Float64, with 2 and with 12 tracers.
  • QA and AD (qa_and_ad.jl): Aqua.test_all, ExplicitImports (Julia ≥ 1.11), no undocumented names, doctests, first and second derivatives of a coupled leaf + stem + column + moist-air pipeline against BigFloat central differences, BigFloat duals, container promotion, value equality and hashing, OffsetArray and view columns, zero-allocation kernels.

Opt-in device scripts build their own temporary environment: test/run_cuda.jl (CuArray broadcasts, CUDA.allowscalar(false)) and test/run_fields.jl (ClimaCore.Fields.Field on CPU and CUDA, Float32 and Float64). GPU arithmetic may contract a*b + c, so sums are compared to rounding tolerance there and pure products exactly.
