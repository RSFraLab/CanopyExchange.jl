Validation & Bit Parity

1. Canonical Extraction & IEEE-754 Bit Parity

CanopyExchange was extracted from CanopyColumn.ConservativeExchange (05723a9eec3baa96da7185dd164a88d622ee72d1) and subsequently extended in v0.1.1 with continuous-scalar {FT<:Real} automatic differentiation, StemFlux / PlantLayerGeometry, and CliMA boundary/tendency contracts.

Homogeneous primal Float32 and Float64 evaluations preserve exact IEEE-754 bit parity with the original donor implementation:

  • test/write_arithmetic_record.jl canonical <out.toml> verifies that all primal Float32 and Float64 bitstrings across computed and absent oxygen match the locked SHA-256 digest 19bcfb73ba7adb1e88f285bbc2679182a861902729216e4948b9d3d350f0d08d.
  • Unary negation uses -field rather than zero(FT) - field, preserving IEEE -0.0 donor sign bits on empty layers.
  • Scalar division uses fieldwise / α rather than * inv(α), preserving exact identity at gas_fraction == 1 and zero-ULP molar O₂ / CO₂ stoichiometry.

2. Continuous-Scalar AD, QA & Device Verification Gates

Pkg.test() (test/runtests.jl) executes deterministic quality-assurance and numerical invariants in a clean sandboxed test environment:

  • Aqua.test_all(CanopyExchange): zero method ambiguities, zero unbound type parameters (including Base.eltype and Base.promote_rule on Union{}), zero undefined exports, zero piracies, and clean Project.toml [compat].
  • ExplicitImports.jl (Julia $\ge 1.11$): passes all seven checks (check_no_implicit_imports, check_no_stale_explicit_imports, check_all_explicit_imports_via_owners, check_all_explicit_imports_are_public, check_all_qualified_accesses_via_owners, check_all_qualified_accesses_are_public, and check_no_self_qualified_accesses).
  • Documentation & doctests: zero Docs.undocumented_names(CanopyExchange) across exported and public bindings, and Documenter.doctest(CanopyExchange) verified on both Pkg.test() and docs/make.jl (checkdocs = :all).
  • 1st- and 2nd-order ForwardDiff vs. BigFloat: end-to-end gradients and Hessians of coupled PlantLayerGeometry + plant_air_sources + steady_top_flux + layer_tendency + moist_air_tendencies pipelines match BigFloat central differences (rtol = 1e-11 gradients, rtol = 1e-9 Hessians), while rejecting NaN/Inf in dual partials via @generated CanopyExchange.finite_scalar.
  • Generic indexing & zero allocations: accumulate_steady_fluxes! and scalar kernels allocate 0 bytes after warmup and support OffsetArray, @view, and 0-layer columns.
  • GPU & CliMA co-load verification: verified on NVIDIA L40S GPUs (test/run_cuda.jl, CUDA.allowscalar(false)) in Float32 and Float64, and co-loaded with ClimaAtmos, ClimaCore, SurfaceFluxes, and Thermodynamics (ClimaCore.Fields.Field @. broadcasting in Float32 and Float64).
