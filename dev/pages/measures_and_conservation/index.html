Measures & Conservation

CanopyExchange enforces measure safety and discrete finite-volume conservation with immutable, isbits records whose scalars share one floating-point type.

1. Conserved quantities and passive tracers (ExchangeRates)

ExchangeRates holds, per second and per measure of the enclosing record:

  1. energy [$\mathrm{J\,s^{-1}}$]: total energy transfer under one caller-declared thermodynamic datum, including the enthalpy carried by transported water vapour. No second latent-heat term may be added when coupling to a conserved energy budget.
  2. water [$\mathrm{kg\,H_2O\,s^{-1}}$]: total water mass transfer.
  3. tracers [$\mathrm{mol\,s^{-1}}$]: passive molar tracers in a NamedTuple, for example (; co2, o2) or (; co2, cos, ch4). CO₂ is in mol CO₂ (one mol C per mol CO₂); photosynthetic uptake from air into the leaf is a negative co2 in a LeafFlux, and condensation onto a surface a negative water.

Host adapters own the conversions between conserved density tendencies and temperature or humidity.

Energy and water are named fields because two operations treat them specially: the water-energy datum shift and the total-mass source of the host (equal to the water source with zero dry-air mass source). Everything else applies componentwise to the tracers present. The tracer rules:

  • Tracers are molar and passive; they carry no energy and no mass in these records. Leaving their mass out of the total-mass source is the trace approximation. With the test fixture's leaf values (12 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ against 4·10⁻⁵ kg H₂O m⁻² s⁻¹), $M_{\mathrm{CO_2}} S_{\mathrm{CO_2}}$ is about 1.3 % of the water mass source, about 0.3 % net when the opposite O₂ flux is counted, and it dominates when transpiration is near zero (at night).
  • Water isotopologues are subsets of water; as tracers they never enter total mass.
  • Bulk O₂ is not a trace constituent. tracers.o2 is a transfer ledger; it becomes a prognostic oxygen budget only if the host has a separate oxygen store contract.
  • A tracer that is not computed is not a key. Records with and without it are different types and cannot be combined (ArgumentError), so an uncomputed tracer can never masquerade as a measured zero. Conversely, a key with value zero (for example (; co2 = 0.0) on a dry stem) asserts a zero transfer; it does not mean "not computed".
  • Tracer order is part of the type.

2. Measures (Exchange{M})

RecordMeasurePositive sign
LeafFlux = Exchange{LeafArea}m² one-sided leaf arealeaf → air
StemFlux = Exchange{StemArea}m² one-sided stem areastem → air
FaceFlux = Exchange{GroundArea}m² horizontal ground areaupward
VolumeSource = Exchange{BulkVolume}m³ bulk canopy volumeinto the named reservoir

Arithmetic exists only within one measure; LeafFlux + StemFlux and LeafFlux + FaceFlux are MethodErrors. A new element class is a new Measure subtype; see section 5.

3. Paired area-to-volume ledgers

A layer geometry is any type with CanopyExchange.thickness [m] and CanopyExchange.area_density [m² m⁻³] for each element class it holds (the interface is documented in one place, the area_density docstring). The package provides LayerGeometry (leaves) and PlantLayerGeometry (leaves and stems; plant_area_density $= \mathrm{LAD}_k + \mathrm{SAD}_k$).

volume_source multiplies an element flux by its own area density once. leaf_air_sources returns (air = +S_leaf, leaf = -S_leaf) with IEEE sign-bit negation; plant_air_sources scales leaf and stem fluxes independently and returns (air, leaf, stem) with

\[S_{\mathrm{air},k} + \left(S_{\mathrm{leaf},k} + S_{\mathrm{stem},k}\right) = 0\]

exactly, with the parentheses as written; other groupings are zero to rounding.

Several populations of one element (sunlit and shaded leaves, cohorts) share LeafArea: each flux is per m² of its own leaf area, so the layer source is $\sum_c$ volume_source(F_c, LayerGeometry(Δz, LAD_c)). Adding the LeafFluxes of different populations without their area weights is wrong. Each donor ledger is the exact negative of its own contribution; it records transfer out of the plant surface, not plant gas storage.

4. Empty layers and derivatives

Layers with zero foliage or zero woody area often hold NaN or Inf dummy rates. area_source asks CanopyExchange.exact_zero:

  • Exact zero density (value and every derivative component zero): an exact zero, without contamination by 0 * NaN, on CPU, CuArray and ClimaCore fields.
  • Perturbed zero density (ForwardDiff.Dual(0.0, 1.0)): rate * area_density, so the right derivative $\partial(\text{rate}\cdot a)/\partial a|_{a=0^+} = \text{rate}$ is kept (requires a finite rate).

5. Extension without changing the package

A third element class (for example fruit) needs a Measure subtype FruitArea, a geometry type with a thickness (field or method) and CanopyExchange.area_density(g, ::Type{FruitArea}), and fluxes built with sensible_water_flux(FruitArea(), H, E, h_v, tracers) (which enforces the vapour enthalpy) or Exchange{FruitArea}(rates). volume_source, layer_tendency and both steady-column functions then work unchanged; Base.eltype is not needed. Define Base.Broadcast.broadcastable(g) = Ref(g) to use the geometry as a scalar in broadcasts. The package cannot validate such a geometry: it must itself guarantee a finite positive thickness and finite nonnegative area densities. The test suite (test/test_extension_points.jl) does this and also carries a second tracer set (co2, cos, ch4) through the records.

6. Telescoping finite-volume divergence

Across $N$ stacked layers with shared upward-positive face fluxes $F_1, \dots, F_{N+1}$ (FaceFlux), layer_tendency evaluates

\[\left(\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}\right)_{\mathrm{bulk},k} = \frac{F_k - F_{k+1}}{\Delta z_k} + S_{\mathrm{air},k},\]

and the column integral cancels every interior face:

\[\sum_{k=1}^N \Delta z_k \left(\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}\right)_{\mathrm{bulk},k} = F_1 - F_{N+1} + \sum_{k=1}^N \Delta z_k \, S_{\mathrm{air},k}.\]
