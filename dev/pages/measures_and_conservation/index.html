Measures & Conservation

CanopyExchange enforces dimensional measure safety and discrete finite-volume conservation through immutable, isbitstype wrappers parameterized on a continuous scalar type FT <: Real.

1. Conserved Budget Quantities (ExchangeRates)

ExchangeRates bundles four extensively conserved transfer rates per second and per wrapper spatial denominator:

  1. energy [$\mathrm{J\,s^{-1}}$ per measure]: total energy transfer under a caller-declared thermodynamic datum (not sensible or latent heat in isolation).
  2. water [$\mathrm{kg\,H_2O\,s^{-1}}$ per measure]: signed water mass transfer.
  3. carbon [$\mathrm{mol\,C\,s^{-1}}$ per measure]: signed molar $\mathrm{CO_2}$ transfer ($1\,\mathrm{mol\,C}$ per $\mathrm{mol\,CO_2}$; photosynthetic uptake from air into leaf is negative in LeafFlux).
  4. oxygen (OxygenContent): either NoOxygen (oxygen_value === nothing, zero storage overhead) or OxygenTransfer [$\mathrm{mol\,O_2\,s^{-1}}$ per measure].

Adding or combining NoOxygen with OxygenTransfer throws MethodError, so an uncomputed oxygen budget can never silently masquerade as zero physical $\mathrm{O_2}$ exchange.

2. Paired Area-to-Volume Ledgers (LAD and SAD)

Canopy layers hold finite positive thickness $\Delta z_k > 0$ [$\mathrm{m}$] and nonnegative one-sided area densities:

  • LayerGeometry: leaf-only layers with leaf_area_density ($\mathrm{LAD}_k \ge 0$).
  • PlantLayerGeometry: coupled foliage and woody layers with leaf_area_density ($\mathrm{LAD}_k \ge 0$) and stem_area_density ($\mathrm{SAD}_k \ge 0$), where plant_area_density returns $\mathrm{PAI}_k / \Delta z_k = \mathrm{LAD}_k + \mathrm{SAD}_k$.

leaf_air_sources multiplies a LeafFlux by $\mathrm{LAD}_k$ once and returns (air = +S_leaf, leaf = -S_leaf) using unary IEEE-754 sign-bit negation. plant_air_sources scales leaf_flux by $\mathrm{LAD}_k$ and stem_flux by $\mathrm{SAD}_k$ independently, returning (air, leaf, stem) such that:

\[S_{\mathrm{air},k} + \left(S_{\mathrm{leaf},k} + S_{\mathrm{stem},k}\right) = 0.\]

The negative donor ledgers (leaf, stem) record instantaneous transfer out of the plant surface rather than asserting internal leaf gas storage.

3. Empty-Layer Nonfinite Guards vs. Directional AD Tangents

In batched CPU and GPU arrays, layers with zero foliage ($\mathrm{LAD}_k = 0$) or zero woody area ($\mathrm{SAD}_k = 0$) frequently hold NaN or Inf sentinel rates in unused slots. area_source inspects CanopyExchange.exact_zero_scalar:

  • Exact zero density (primal and all AD partials equal zero): returns exact zero(FT) without multiplying rate, preventing 0 * NaN == NaN contamination on CPU, CuArray, and ClimaCore.Fields.Field kernels.
  • Perturbed zero density (ForwardDiff.Dual(0.0, 1.0)): evaluates rate * area_density directly (requiring finite rate) so the right directional derivative $\partial(\text{rate}\cdot a)/\partial a\big|_{a=0^+} = \text{rate}$ is preserved.

4. Telescoping Finite-Volume Divergence

Across $N$ stacked layers with shared upward-positive horizontal interface fluxes $F_{1}, \dots, F_{N+1}$ (FaceFlux), layer_tendency evaluates:

\[\left(\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}\right)_{\mathrm{bulk},k} = \frac{F_k - F_{k+1}}{\Delta z_k} + S_{\mathrm{air},k}.\]

Integrating over the column $\sum_{k=1}^N \Delta z_k (\partial_t \psi)_{\mathrm{bulk},k}$ cancels every interior face flux $F_2, \dots, F_N$ identically:

\[\sum_{k=1}^N \Delta z_k \left(\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t}\right)_{\mathrm{bulk},k} = F_1 - F_{N+1} + \sum_{k=1}^N \Delta z_k \, S_{\mathrm{air},k}.\]
