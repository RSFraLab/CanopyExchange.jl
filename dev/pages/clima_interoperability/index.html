CliMA Interoperability

CanopyExchange is designed to compose directly with CliMA packages (Thermodynamics.jl, SurfaceFluxes.jl, ClimaLand.jl, ClimaAtmos.jl, and ClimaCore.jl) with no runtime dependencies in its own Project.toml (ForwardDiff is an optional weak dependency that only loads the scalar-hook extension).

1. SurfaceFluxes.jl & Thermodynamics.jl Static-Energy Datum

In SurfaceFluxes.jl, surface sensible and latent heat fluxes (sfc.shf, sfc.lhf) and the evaporation mass flux (sfc.evaporation, $E$ [$\mathrm{kg\,m^{-2}\,s^{-1}}$]) are defined relative to Thermodynamics.jl's reference temperature $T_0$ and surface geopotential $\Phi_{\mathrm{sfc}} = g z_{\mathrm{sfc}}$:

\[\begin{aligned} \mathrm{SHF} &= -\rho_{\mathrm{sfc}} g_h ( \mathrm{DSE}_{\mathrm{int}} - \mathrm{DSE}_{\mathrm{sfc}} ) + \mathrm{VSE}_{\mathrm{sfc}} \, E, \\ \mathrm{LHF} &= \mathrm{LH}_{v0} \, E, \end{aligned}\]

where $\mathrm{VSE}_{\mathrm{sfc}} = c_{pv}(T_{\mathrm{sfc}} - T_0) + \Phi_{\mathrm{sfc}}$ is Thermodynamics.vapor_static_energy(thermo_params, T_sfc, Φ_sfc). Summing both components yields the exact moist static-energy boundary flux:

\[\mathrm{SHF} + \mathrm{LHF} = -\rho_{\mathrm{sfc}} g_h \Delta\mathrm{DSE} + \bigl(h_v(T_{\mathrm{sfc}}) + \Phi_{\mathrm{sfc}}\bigr) E,\]

because $\mathrm{LH}_{v0} + c_{pv}(T_{\mathrm{sfc}} - T_0) = h_v(T_{\mathrm{sfc}})$ (Thermodynamics.enthalpy_vapor(thermo_params, T_sfc)). This holds for fluxes computed by SurfaceFluxes.jl ≥ 1.0, not for prescribed fluxes. Pass sfc.shf, sfc.lhf, and sfc.evaporation (the evaporation mass flux) directly to surface_static_energy_flux:

# With sfc = SurfaceFluxes.surface_conditions(sf_params, sf_inputs):
soil_flux = surface_static_energy_flux(sfc.shf, sfc.lhf, sfc.evaporation, (; co2 = soil_co2_flux))

For leaf and stem layers at height $z_k$ ($\Phi_k = g z_k$), pass Thermodynamics.enthalpy_vapor(thermo_params, T_leaf) for a pure enthalpy budget ($\Phi = 0$), or Thermodynamics.enthalpy_vapor(thermo_params, T_leaf) + Φ_k when coupling to a total moist energy budget ($\rho e_{\mathrm{tot}}$) that includes gravitational potential energy:

hv_k = Thermodynamics.enthalpy_vapor(thermo_params, T_leaf) + grav * z_k
lf_k = sensible_water_flux(H_leaf, E_leaf, hv_k, (; co2 = C_leaf))

2. Reference-Enthalpy & Geopotential Shifts (shift_water_energy_datum)

Eddy-covariance flux products and standalone leaf models often report $H + L_v(T_{\mathrm{air}})\,E$: latent heat at the air temperature and no geopotential. Thermodynamics.jl's energy datum is liquid water at the triple point $T_0 = 273.16\,\mathrm{K}$, and the static-energy boundary of SurfaceFluxes.jl carries $E\,(h_v(T_{\mathrm{sfc}}) + \Phi)$. The shift per kg of water that maps the eddy-covariance form onto that datum is

\[\delta = h_v(T_{\mathrm{sfc}}) + \Phi - L_v(T_{\mathrm{air}}) \approx c_l\,(T_{\mathrm{air}} - T_0) + \Phi ,\]

because $h_v(T) = L_{v,0} + c_{pv}(T - T_0)$ and $L_v(T) = L_{v,0} + (c_{pv} - c_l)(T - T_0)$ differ by $c_l (T - T_0)$ (the approximation takes $T_{\mathrm{sfc}} \approx T_{\mathrm{air}}$; otherwise add $c_{pv}(T_{\mathrm{sfc}} - T_{\mathrm{air}})$). Here $c_l$ and $c_{pv}$ are the specific heats of liquid water and water vapour.

Use shift_water_energy_datum with $\Delta e_w = e_{w,\mathrm{target}} - e_{w,\mathrm{source}}$ [$\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,H_2O}$] on individual shared FaceFlux, LeafFlux, StemFlux, or VolumeSource streams prior to layer_tendency:

julia> using CanopyExchange

julia> faces = [face_flux(120.0, 4.0e-5, (; co2 = 0.0)), face_flux(150.0, 5.0e-5, (; co2 = 0.0))];

julia> z_faces = [0.0, 2.0]; grav = 9.80665;

julia> faces_geo = shift_water_energy_datum.(faces, grav .* z_faces);

julia> faces_geo[2].rates.energy - faces[2].rates.energy ≈ grav * 2.0 * 5.0e-5
true

Applying the shift to shared faces before taking finite-volume differences guarantees that interior face fluxes cancel identically across layer interfaces; shifting a layer's net divergence after layer_tendency would drop the vertical gradient of $\Phi(z)$ across the cell.

3. Diagnostic Steady-Air Columns (ClimaLand Bridge)

Big-leaf and diagnostic multi-layer land models neglect canopy-air storage ($\partial_t \psi_{\mathrm{air}} = 0$) so the upward canopy-top flux equals soil boundary flux plus vertically integrated plant sources. steady_top_flux, accumulate_steady_fluxes!, and accumulate_steady_fluxes bridge diagnostic and prognostic formulations directly:

julia> using CanopyExchange

julia> geoms = [PlantLayerGeometry(1.5, 0.0, 0.2), PlantLayerGeometry(2.5, 1.4, 0.15)];

julia> lfs = [sensible_water_flux(NaN, NaN, 2.5e6, (; co2 = NaN)), sensible_water_flux(90.0, 3.5e-5, 2.5e6, (; co2 = -1.2e-5))];

julia> sfs = [sensible_stem_flux(25.0, (; co2 = 0.0)), sensible_stem_flux(15.0, (; co2 = 0.0))];

julia> air_srcs = getproperty.(plant_air_sources.(lfs, sfs, geoms), :air);

julia> soil = surface_static_energy_flux(40.0, 80.0, 3.2e-5, (; co2 = 2.0e-6));

julia> faces = accumulate_steady_fluxes(soil, air_srcs, geoms);

julia> maximum(k -> abs(layer_tendency(faces[k], faces[k + 1], air_srcs[k], geoms[k]).rates.energy), eachindex(geoms)) <= 1.0e-13
true

4. ClimaAtmos & ClimaCore.Fields.Field Porous-Canopy Tendencies

Prognostic atmospheric columns with time-invariant air porosity $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{gas}} \in (0, 1]$ ($\partial_t \varepsilon_{\mathrm{gas}} = 0$) and conserved dry-air mass ($S_{\rho_d} = 0$) evolve conserved densities per unit gas volume. moist_air_tendencies divides bulk-volume tendencies fieldwise by gas_fraction, returning MoistAirTendencies with rho_total == rho_water == water / ε_gas, rho_energy == energy / ε_gas, and every tracer divided by ε_gas. Tracers stay nested in tracers, so a tracer name cannot overwrite a host field.

Every CanopyExchange record is a scalar in broadcasts and every kernel takes positional arguments only, without heap allocations, so column pipelines broadcast over ClimaCore.Fields.Field and CuArray. GPU field broadcasts reject a type or a keyword argument in the function position, so construct records with the function forms (layer_geometry, leaf_flux, stem_flux, face_flux) and the Tracers callable (test/run_fields.jl runs this on CPU and CUDA fields):

# On a ClimaCore CenterFiniteDifferenceSpace:
T       = Tracers(:co2)
air(p)  = p.air
geom_f  = layer_geometry.(dz_f, lad_f, sad_f)
lf_f    = sensible_water_flux.(sh_leaf_f, evap_leaf_f, hv_f, T.(co2_leaf_f))
sf_f    = sensible_stem_flux.(sh_stem_f, T.(co2_stem_f))
air_f   = air.(plant_air_sources.(lf_f, sf_f, geom_f))
tend_f  = layer_tendency.(bot_face_f, top_face_f, air_f, geom_f)
moist_f = moist_air_tendencies.(tend_f, eps_gas_f)
