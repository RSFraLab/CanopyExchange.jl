Area Sources & Finite-Volume Divergence

Element fluxes become bulk-volume sources through one multiplication by their own area density, with paired donor ledgers; face fluxes and sources become conservative layer tendencies.

Element area to bulk volume

CanopyExchange.area_source — Function
area_source(rate::Real, area_density::Real)

Convert a scalar transfer per m² one-sided element area and second into a source per m³ bulk canopy volume and second by multiplying by area_density [m² m⁻³] once. Both arguments are scalars; records go through volume_source, which keeps their measure tags.

Empty-layer rule: when area_density is an exact zero (value and every derivative component zero, see CanopyExchange.exact_zero) the result is an exact zero, even when the unused element rate is NaN or Inf. A zero value with a nonzero derivative component (Dual(0.0, 1.0)) evaluates rate * area_density, which keeps the right derivative ∂(rate a)/∂a = rate and requires a finite rate.

Examples

julia> area_source(150.0, 0.8)
120.0

julia> area_source(NaN, 0.0)
0.0
CanopyExchange.volume_source — Function
volume_source(flux::Exchange{M}, geometry) -> VolumeSource

Volume source of one element class: every component of flux times the area density CanopyExchange.area_density(geometry, M) of its own class, through area_source (so the empty-layer rule applies). Positive into canopy air. A flux of a class the geometry does not hold is a MethodError; a FaceFlux or a VolumeSource is not an element flux.

Examples

julia> s = volume_source(leaf_flux(100.0, 2.0e-5, (; co2 = -1.0e-5)), LayerGeometry(2.0, 0.5));

julia> (s.rates.energy, s.rates.tracers.co2)
(50.0, -5.0e-6)
CanopyExchange.leaf_area_source — Function
leaf_area_source(leaf_rate::Real, geometry)

area_source of one scalar leaf rate [per m² leaf s⁻¹] with the leaf area density of geometry: a bulk-volume source [per m³ s⁻¹], positive into canopy air. Negate it for the leaf donor ledger.

Examples

julia> leaf_area_source(2.0e-5, LayerGeometry(1.5, 0.5))
1.0e-5
CanopyExchange.stem_area_source — Function
stem_area_source(stem_rate::Real, geometry)

area_source of one scalar stem rate [per m² stem s⁻¹] with the stem area density of geometry. A geometry without stems is a MethodError.

Examples

julia> stem_area_source(40.0, PlantLayerGeometry(2.0, 1.2, 0.25))
10.0

Paired leaf, stem and canopy-air ledgers

CanopyExchange.leaf_air_sources — Function
leaf_air_sources(leaf::LeafFlux, geometry) -> (; air, leaf)

Paired VolumeSource ledgers of a leaf layer: air is the leaf flux times the leaf area density (volume_source), and the donor ledger leaf is its exact IEEE negation, so air + leaf is exactly zero componentwise. The donor ledger records transfer out of the leaf surface; it is not leaf gas storage or a leaf temperature tendency. An empty layer returns exact zeros even for nonfinite dummy leaf rates.

Examples

julia> p = leaf_air_sources(leaf_flux(100.0, 2.0e-5, (; co2 = -1.0e-5)), LayerGeometry(2.0, 0.5));

julia> (p.air.rates.energy, p.leaf.rates.energy)
(50.0, -50.0)
CanopyExchange.plant_air_sources — Function
plant_air_sources(leaf::LeafFlux, stem::StemFlux, geometry) -> (; air, leaf, stem)

Paired ledgers of a leaf-and-stem layer. The leaf flux is scaled by the leaf area density and the stem flux by the stem area density, each with its own empty-layer rule. air is the sum of both contributions; leaf and stem are the exact negations of their own contributions. Hence air + (leaf + stem) is exactly zero (the sum leaf + stem is the exact negation of air), while other groupings such as (air + leaf) + stem are zero only to rounding. Leaf and stem fluxes must carry the same tracers. A geometry without stems is a MethodError.

Examples

julia> lf = leaf_flux(100.0, 2.0e-5, (; co2 = -1.0e-5));

julia> sf = sensible_stem_flux(40.0, (; co2 = 0.0));

julia> s = plant_air_sources(lf, sf, PlantLayerGeometry(2.0, 1.0, 0.25));

julia> (s.air.rates.energy, s.leaf.rates.energy, s.stem.rates.energy)
(110.0, -100.0, -10.0)

Finite-volume vertical divergence

CanopyExchange.conserved_tendency — Function
conserved_tendency(bottom::Real, top::Real, source::Real, geometry)

Finite-volume tendency (bottom - top) / thickness + source of one conserved scalar. bottom and top are upward-positive face fluxes per m² ground area and second; source and the result are per m³ bulk canopy volume and second. Records go through layer_tendency, which keeps their measure tags. Summing thickness * tendency over stacked layers cancels every interior face flux.

Examples

julia> conserved_tendency(15.0, 12.0, 4.0, LayerGeometry(1.5, 0.8))
6.0
CanopyExchange.layer_tendency — Function
layer_tendency(bottom::FaceFlux, top::FaceFlux, source::VolumeSource, geometry) -> VolumeSource

Bulk-volume tendency (bottom - top) / thickness + source of every component (conserved_tendency applied componentwise). Face fluxes are upward positive per m² ground area; all records must carry the same tracers and precision. Summing thickness * tendency over adjacent layers telescopes the interior faces. Apply any datum shift to the individual streams before this call (shift_water_energy_datum).

Examples

julia> b, t = face_flux(30.0, 0.0), face_flux(24.0, 0.0);

julia> layer_tendency(b, t, VolumeSource(ExchangeRates(10.0, 0.0)), LayerGeometry(2.0, 0.5)).rates.energy
13.0
